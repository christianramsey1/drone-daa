Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

DroneDAA ("we", "our", "the app") is published by CAVU Consulting LLC on behalf of uAvionix Corporation. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

Device location (optional). The app may request your location to center the map on your position and calculate proximity alerts. Location data is processed on-device only — it is never transmitted to our servers, stored remotely, or shared with third parties.

Bluetooth data. The app uses Bluetooth to scan for nearby drones broadcasting FAA Remote ID signals. This data is processed on-device only.

Local network data. The app connects to ADS-B receivers on your local network to receive aircraft position data via the GDL-90 protocol. This data is processed on-device only.

App preferences. Settings such as alert thresholds, map preferences, and saved locations are stored locally on your device using standard device storage.

Usage analytics. We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information, account data, or email addresses.
  • We do not track your location history.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or serve ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not use cookies or web tracking.
  • We do not transmit any data to our servers — all processing happens on your device.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Map display: Your location is used to center the map on your position.
  • Proximity alerts: Your location and nearby aircraft/drone positions are used to calculate detect-and-avoid alerts.
  • Airspace awareness: FAA airspace data is fetched from public FAA services based on the map viewport.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Apple MapKit JS: For map display. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple WeatherKit: For weather data.
  • FAA ArcGIS Services: For airspace boundary data (publicly available, no personal data sent).
  • USGS The National Map: For topographic map tiles (public U.S. government service; map tile requests are not linked to any user identity).

5. Data Storage and Security

DroneDAA does not store any personal data on remote servers. All app data (settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Weather and airspace data are fetched from public APIs and are not linked to any user identity.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no user data to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about your privacy, contact us at the email below.
